▾ Need help? Detailed browser guide & console fallback
How to use the Console Fallback (if bookmark is blocked): 1. Click Copy script (console fallback) above.
2. On your Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok chat tab, press F12 (or right click → Inspect).
3. Switch to the Console tab, press Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V), and press Enter.
Browser Specific Help: • Google Chrome / Brave / Edge: Ensure the bookmarks bar is visible (Ctrl+Shift+B). Click and drag the button directly into the bar.
• Firefox: Right-click your bookmarks toolbar → click Bookmarks Toolbar → Always Show, then drag the button.
• Safari: Show Favorites Bar (Cmd+Shift+B), drag the link into your Favorites.
Privacy & Security: The bookmarklet is strictly read-only and open-source. It does not read cookies, local storage, or credentials. Data leaves your browser only when you click Open in Context Exporter.
